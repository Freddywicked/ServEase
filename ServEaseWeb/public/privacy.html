Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

ServEase ("we", "our", "the app") connects customers with local service providers. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the ServEase mobile app and the ServEase website.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, mobile phone number, address, birthdate, and gender, provided when you register directly or sign in with Google (Google sign-in shares only your name, email address, and profile picture with us; it never shares your Google password).
  • Phone verification — your mobile number is verified with a one-time SMS code.
  • Location — approximate or precise device location, only when you grant permission, used to find nearby service providers and set your service radius.
  • Service requests — descriptions, photos, preferred schedules, and messages you exchange with providers.
  • Provider verification documents — for service providers only: government ID, selfie, and supporting certifications submitted for account verification.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by PayMongo (GCash, QR Ph, cards). We never see or store your full card or wallet details; we only store the payment status and reference numbers.
  • Device token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to deliver push notifications about your requests.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account (including Google sign-in and SMS verification).
  • Match you with nearby service providers and manage your service requests.
  • Send notifications and messages about your bookings.
  • Verify service providers before they appear in search results.
  • Process payments and refunds through PayMongo.
  • Improve the service, including AI-assisted diagnosis of your service request.

3. Third-party services

We share the minimum necessary data with:

  • Google — sign-in (OAuth) and Maps/location services.
  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications.
  • Supabase — database hosting.
  • PhilSMS — SMS delivery of verification codes (your phone number only).
  • PayMongo — payment processing.
  • Google Gemini — AI-assisted diagnosis of service request descriptions.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You may request account deletion or a copy of your data at any time by emailing us at the address below. Verification documents of rejected provider applications are removed after review.

5. Security

Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes, sessions use signed tokens, and all traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

6. Children

ServEase is not intended for users under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
